Your hotel sells a room for $100 on Booking.com. Could you offer the same room for $95 on your own website?

The answer depends on your location, your OTA agreements and applicable competition rules.

**Hotel rate parity** refers to arrangements that restrict accommodation providers from offering different prices or booking conditions across certain distribution channels.

For independent hotels, guesthouses and villas, understanding these restrictions is important when developing a direct-booking strategy.

## What Is Hotel Rate Parity?

Rate parity describes the relationship between prices offered through different booking channels.

There are two commonly discussed arrangements:

### Wide rate parity

Wide parity generally restricts a hotel from offering better prices through competing distribution channels, potentially including its own website.

### Narrow rate parity

Narrow parity generally focuses on the hotel's own direct online sales channels. Restrictions may apply to publicly advertised direct rates while allowing greater flexibility across competing OTAs.

Actual restrictions depend on the agreement and applicable regulations.

**Rate parity is not a universal law requiring every hotel to display identical prices everywhere.**

## Can Hotels Offer Cheaper Direct Booking Rates?

In some markets, yes.

An important development concerns Booking.com and the European Economic Area (EEA).

Under the European Union's Digital Markets Act (DMA), Booking.com became subject to obligations restricting certain practices that prevent business users from offering different prices or conditions through other channels.

These obligations have applied since November 14, 2024.

This means accommodation providers covered by these rules can offer better prices or conditions through their own websites.

However, these European requirements do not automatically apply worldwide.

For example, a guesthouse in Sri Lanka should not assume that EEA regulations override the terms applicable to its Booking.com account.

Before introducing direct-booking discounts:

- Review your current OTA agreements.
- Check the rules applicable to your property.
- Compare the full booking conditions, not just the advertised price.

## Why Direct Bookings Can Be More Profitable

OTAs provide valuable exposure, especially for independent properties that may struggle to reach international travellers independently.

However, OTA reservations can involve commissions and other distribution costs.

Direct bookings can avoid certain intermediary charges, but they still involve expenses such as:

- Website hosting and maintenance
- Booking-engine subscriptions
- Payment processing
- Advertising and marketing
- Customer support

The important metric is not simply the room price. It is how much revenue the property retains after acquiring the booking.

### Example: OTA vs Direct Booking

Consider a hypothetical hotel selling a room for $100.

Assume the OTA booking has a 15% commission. The direct booking offers a $5 discount, with $3 payment processing and $5 marketing costs.

| Item | OTA | Direct |
|---|---:|---:|
| Room price | $100 | $95 |
| OTA commission | $15 | $0 |
| Payment processing | $0* | $3 |
| Marketing cost | $0* | $5 |
| Revenue after listed costs | $85 | $87 |

*Costs not separately included in this simplified OTA example.*

Despite offering the guest a $5 discount, the hotel retains $2 more from the direct reservation under these assumptions.

These figures are illustrative, not industry averages. Actual results depend on the hotel's acquisition and operating costs.

## Five Ways to Encourage Direct Bookings

You don't necessarily need to offer cheaper room rates to attract direct reservations.

Here are five practical approaches.

### 1. Offer Additional Benefits

Depending on your agreements and applicable rules, consider:

- Complimentary early check-in when available
- Late checkout
- Welcome drinks
- Room upgrades subject to availability

These benefits can make direct bookings more attractive without necessarily changing advertised room prices.

### 2. Improve Your Booking Website

Make room information, photographs, amenities, cancellation policies and prices easy to understand.

Ensure the booking process works properly on mobile devices.

A complicated booking experience can send potential guests back to OTAs.

### 3. Make Payment Conditions Clear

Clearly communicate payment requirements, cancellation deadlines, taxes and additional charges before checkout.

Guests are more likely to trust a booking process when they understand exactly what they are paying for.

### 4. Encourage Returning Guests

Previous guests may be more comfortable booking directly next time.

Maintain good communication, provide accurate contact information and make your official website easy to find.

### 5. Measure Your Results

Track the performance of each booking channel.

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Direct booking share | Measures reservations made directly |
| ADR | Measures average room revenue |
| Acquisition cost | Measures booking acquisition expenses |
| Cancellation rate | Measures cancelled reservations |
| Revenue after distribution costs | Measures retained revenue |

A smaller number of profitable direct reservations can be more valuable than a larger number acquired through expensive marketing.

## How a Channel Manager Helps

A channel manager distributes rates, availability and restrictions between a property's reservation system and connected booking channels.

This reduces the need to update each OTA manually.

However, a channel manager does not determine which pricing arrangements are legally or contractually permitted.

The hotel remains responsible for its pricing strategy and applicable agreements.

OTAs may also apply their own promotions, currency conversions and eligibility-based discounts, meaning the final prices displayed to guests may differ.

For smaller properties, maintaining accurate inventory and understanding each channel's pricing rules are essential.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is hotel rate parity illegal?

There is no universal answer. Some jurisdictions restrict particular parity clauses, while others permit certain contractual arrangements. The applicable rules depend on the market and agreement.

### Can I offer cheaper rates on my hotel website than on Booking.com?

It may be permitted, depending on your location, account terms and applicable regulations. Always review your current agreement before changing public rates.

### Do direct bookings always make more money?

No. Direct bookings can avoid OTA commissions but may involve advertising, payment processing and technology costs. Compare the actual contribution from each booking.

### Can a channel manager maintain rate parity?

A channel manager can synchronize configured rates across supported channels. However, platform promotions, taxes and currency conversion may still create differences in displayed prices.

## Final Thoughts

Hotel rate parity is about more than keeping identical prices across booking websites.

It involves contractual obligations, competition rules, distribution costs and the financial value of each reservation.

For independent hotels, guesthouses and villas, the goal should be to build a balanced distribution strategy.

Use OTAs to reach new travellers while improving your direct-booking experience and understanding the costs associated with every reservation.
