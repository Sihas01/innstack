Your hotel has 12 rooms booked for next Saturday. Is that good or bad?

The answer depends on how quickly reservations are arriving and how much demand you normally receive.

A **hotel booking pickup report** tracks changes in future reservations over a specific period. It helps independent hotels, guesthouses and villas identify strong and weak dates before guests arrive.

## What Is Hotel Booking Pickup?

Booking pickup measures how many additional room nights are booked for a future stay date between two reporting points.

For example, if your hotel had 10 rooms reserved for next Saturday on Monday and 14 on Friday, your net pickup is four room nights.

**Booking pickup formula:**

Net Booking Pickup = Room Nights on the Books Now − Room Nights on the Books Previously

Alternatively, calculate net pickup from new bookings, cancellations and reservation modifications affecting the selected stay dates.

Pickup can also be measured using room revenue rather than room nights.

### Pickup vs Occupancy

These metrics answer different questions.

| Metric | What It Measures |
|---|---|
| Occupancy | Percentage of available room nights occupied or reserved |
| Booking pickup | Change in reservations between reporting dates |
| Booking pace | How reservations are building compared with a reference period |
| ADR | Average room revenue per sold room night |

Occupancy shows your current booking position. Pickup reveals whether reservations are increasing or decreasing.

Both are useful when deciding whether to adjust prices.

## How to Calculate Booking Pickup

Consider a hypothetical 20-room boutique hotel reviewing reservations for an upcoming Saturday.

| Measurement | Room Nights |
|---|---:|
| Booked last Monday | 11 |
| New bookings during the week | 6 |
| Cancelled room nights | 2 |
| Booked this Monday | 15 |
| Net pickup | +4 |

The calculation is:

**6 new room nights − 2 cancelled room nights = 4 net pickup**

The hotel now has 15 of its 20 rooms reserved, representing 75% booked occupancy for that date.

These figures are illustrative.

Importantly, six new bookings do not mean six additional occupied room nights. Cancellations must also be considered.

A property that tracks only new reservations may overestimate future demand.

## How to Create a Simple Pickup Report

You don't need expensive revenue management software to begin tracking booking pickup.

A spreadsheet can work for a small property.

### Step 1: Choose Future Stay Dates

Start with the next 30 days.

Record the number of room nights already reserved for each date.

### Step 2: Save Regular Snapshots

Record the same information every Monday, or daily if booking activity is high.

Use consistent reservation statuses and inventory definitions.

### Step 3: Calculate Net Changes

Subtract the previous snapshot from the latest one.

Include cancellations and reservation modifications.

### Step 4: Compare Similar Dates

A Saturday during a local festival may behave differently from an ordinary Tuesday.

Compare pickup with relevant historical patterns, seasonal demand and known events.

### Step 5: Review the Results

Look for dates where bookings are arriving faster or slower than expected.

A simple report might look like this:

| Stay Date | Previous Bookings | Current Bookings | Net Pickup |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Friday | 8 | 11 | +3 |
| Saturday | 12 | 17 | +5 |
| Sunday | 7 | 6 | -1 |

*Illustrative room-night figures.*

This report quickly shows where booking momentum is strongest.

## How to Use Pickup Data for Pricing

Booking pickup is most useful when combined with remaining availability, ADR and expected demand.

### When Pickup Is Strong

If reservations are arriving faster than usual and few rooms remain, consider whether your current rates are too low.

You may have an opportunity to increase prices for remaining inventory.

However, avoid raising rates based on one unusually strong day without reviewing the broader booking pattern.

### When Pickup Is Weak

Slow pickup doesn't automatically mean you should discount.

First check whether:

- Your rooms are visible and bookable on connected OTAs.
- Your prices are competitive for the dates.
- Local demand is unusually low.
- Cancellation activity has increased.
- Your direct-booking website works correctly.

Separate new demand from lost reservations with the [cancellation-rate report by channel](/guides/hotel-cancellation-rate-by-booking-channel/). Strong gross bookings can still produce weak net pickup when cancellations rise.

If demand remains weak, consider a targeted offer rather than discounting every date.

### Monitor Revenue, Not Just Room Nights

Five additional reservations at heavily discounted rates may generate less value than fewer reservations at stronger rates.

Compare pickup alongside ADR and projected room revenue.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How often should hotels review booking pickup?

A small property can begin with weekly reviews. Daily monitoring may be more useful during peak seasons or periods of rapid booking activity.

### Is booking pickup the same as booking lead time?

No. Booking lead time measures the time between reservation creation and arrival. Pickup measures how reservations change between reporting snapshots. The [booking lead-time guide](/guides/hotel-booking-lead-time-guide/) explains how to compare booking windows before deciding that pickup is unusually slow.

### Can a small guesthouse use Excel for pickup reports?

Yes. A spreadsheet with stay dates, previous bookings, current bookings and net changes is sufficient for a basic report.

## Final Thoughts

Booking pickup helps hotels understand not only how many rooms are reserved, but how quickly future demand is developing.

For independent properties, a simple weekly report can reveal opportunities to adjust pricing, investigate weak dates and plan operations.

**Start with accurate reservation snapshots, monitor net changes and use pickup alongside occupancy and ADR rather than making decisions from one number alone.**
